- Daily limits: choose how many minutes a day Reels, Shorts or the TikTok feed may stay open. Feedcut warns one minute before the end, then cuts them until midnight.
- Tapping back into a cut screen no longer throws you out of the app.
- Better at catching screens on phones that send few events, such as Android 8.
